# Sent Message Style

Read when: drafting or polishing a message sent to people, such as a Slack,
email, or chat message or a ticket comment. Commit messages, branch names, PR
bodies, issues, and documents such as a README or Design Doc are out of scope.

## Replying

- When replying to a request, write the result of what was asked first, in the
  requester's own words, then ask for the next decision or task.
- When replying to a question, quote the question and answer directly beneath
  it, without a preamble or heading.
- When the recipient asks whether you can do something ("can you ...?"), answer
  whether it can be done and under what constraints.

## Content and Recipients

- Write why something cannot be done, and the planned work on your side, only
  when the recipient needs it for a decision or action.
- Address recipients separately by role, and phrase what a recipient decides as
  a question to them.
- Propose an approach or schedule that adds work for the development team only
  when the requester stated it; when the draft contains one and the materials
  hold no instruction from the requester, list it as a point to confirm.
